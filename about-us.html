Where It Started & How We Grew

A founder-led journey from organic community building to strategic enterprise PR and political marketing execution.

01

Where It Started

I started my digital marketing journey by creating my own Instagram page, @aapno_bhaylu_, which quickly gained a strong local following.

As I explored the potential of social media, I built a community of 250+ pages in the same category by collaborating with other page admins.

This allowed me to introduce bulk page promotions to brands, creating a unique and effective way for businesses to increase their visibility and reach their target audiences.

02

Building Digital Expertise

Through this experience, I expanded my knowledge of digital marketing and went on to build a successful bootstrap startup.
